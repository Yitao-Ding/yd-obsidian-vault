## 原文 (Abstract)

Small businesses start with people who are exceptional at what they do—a craft, a trade, an idea they believe in. But building a business demands far more than expertise alone. With a lean team, limited time, and few resources, every owner is expected to be the marketer, accountant, salesperson, operator, and strategist all-in-one. We believe AI can change that by being a force multiplier that extends individual expertise, increases capacity, and gives everyone access to world class tools they need to achieve their greatest ambitions.
